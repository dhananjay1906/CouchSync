# CouchSync privacy policy (template — edit before publishing)

**What CouchSync does:** syncs play/pause/position of a video you're watching with up to 3 friends, and connects your camera, microphone and chat with them.

**Data that leaves your computer**
- **To the CouchSync server** (`<your server address>`): your display name, a random ID, the party code, the address and title of the video page you're watching (so friends can open it), playback position and play/pause/seek events, chat messages, and technical details needed to connect video calls. The server keeps this only in memory while the party exists (chat: last 50 messages) and deletes it about 10 minutes after everyone leaves. Server logs record party creation/closing times and connection counts.
- **To your friends in the party:** your name, chat messages, playback actions, and your camera/microphone (only when you turn them on). Video and audio go directly between browsers (peer-to-peer), which means friends' browsers can see your IP address — unless you turn on "Hide my IP", in which case it goes through a relay server.
- **To the relay provider** (e.g. Cloudflare), only when a direct connection isn't possible or "Hide my IP" is on: your encrypted audio/video stream.

**What we don't do:** no accounts, no ads, no selling data, no recording of video, audio or chat. Streaming services' video never passes through CouchSync.

**Stored on your computer:** your name, your random ID, server address and settings (in the browser's extension storage).
